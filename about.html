Who We Are

Comprehensive Oral Healthcare Under One Roof

SHIVAM DENTAL AND MAXILLOFACIAL CENTRE is situated at C-1/23, First Floor, Yamuna Vihar, Delhi 110053 (C/o Shivam Ortho & Spine Center).

Our centre was established with a singular vision: to deliver world-class dental treatments and complex maxillofacial surgeries at transparent and accessible prices. With a team of 7 senior MDS specialists with 10 to 20+ years of dedicated clinical experience, we cover every facet of dentistry including single-sitting microscopic root canal treatments, clear aligners, dental implants, pediatric dentistry, and oral surgery.

Digital Diagnostics High-precision intra-oral cameras and digital RVG X-Rays with 90% reduced radiation.
Hospital-Grade Hygiene Strict Class-B autoclave sterilization and single-use disposable kits for 100% patient safety.
