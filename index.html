About Our Centre

Precision Dentistry & Complex Maxillofacial Care

SHIVAM DENTAL AND MAXILLOFACIAL CENTRE (located at Shivam Ortho & Spine Center, Yamuna Vihar, Delhi) is a multidisciplinary specialty dental centre renowned for ethical practice and surgical excellence.

Led by 7 senior doctors with 10 to 20 years of clinical and surgical expertise, our clinic provides single-sitting root canals, clear aligners, dental implants, pediatric dentistry, and complex oral maxillofacial surgeries under one roof.
